Aura

A personal designer in every NRI pocket — the conversational fashion platform built for the global Indian community

IConversational fashionI

Overview

For Indians living abroad, dressing for a wedding back home is a logistics problem dressed up as a shopping one. The outfit has to fit without a trial, clear customs without a surprise bill, and land before the sangeet — usually ordered from a boutique the customer has never met.

Aura is not a catalog. It opens with a conversation — “What are you dressing for?” — and answers with a stylist backed by a workshop of verified artisans. Every screen ladders up to that promise: occasion before object, fit as a guarantee, and every price shown next to a delivery date.

Design principles

Aura is a conversation with a stylist who happens to have a workshop of artisans behind them. Every UX decision had to ladder up to that.

01

Occasion before object

Never open with a grid of products. Open with the moment — a wedding, a Diwali dinner, a temple visit — and let intent drive everything else.

02

Ask, then curate

A few sharp questions earn a small, hand-picked shortlist. Three considered options beat three hundred.

03

Show the human

Boutique names, artisan photos, city of origin and craft heritage are first-class content, not footnotes.

04

Fit is a promise, not a form

Every measurement flow ends with a guarantee. If it doesn’t fit, Aura fixes it — and that reshapes onboarding, checkout and delivery.

05

Time is the hidden currency

Every price lives next to a delivery window; every delivery window lives next to an event date. The biggest fear isn’t cost — it’s missing the occasion.

06

Make transparency the feature

Cutting, embroidery, quality check, dispatch — the customer sees each step with a real photo. Progress is content.

07

Design for two homes

US billing, India delivery, a mother’s measurements, a daughter’s preferences. Data, IA and copy all assume dual geography.

08

Personalization compounds

Every tap, save, return and measurement trains the Fashion Graph. The app should feel like it remembers — because it does.

Seven jobs to be done

Every screen had to advance one of them — if a feature didn’t map to a job, it was cut.

  • Help me look right for this moment
  • Get me a fit I can trust
  • Make my inspiration real
  • Coordinate with my people
  • Get it in my hands on time
  • Prove the craft is real
  • Remember me next time

Information architecture

Aura organizes around Moments (why you shop) and People (who you shop for), not categories. Category browse still exists — one tap below the surface.

Global controls

  • Ship to — persistent in the header; resets shipping and duties everywhere.
  • Currency follows Ship to by default, overridable in Settings.
  • Wearing for — the person selector that decides which measurement profile applies.
  • Deliver by — an event-date pill that filters designers by feasible lead time.

The eight-stage NRI journey

From the moment an invitation arrives to the wedding photo on the fridge — each stage has its own screens, notifications and success metric.

01

Trigger

An invite, a festival on the calendar, a friend’s post. Aura shows up as a push, the occasion calendar, an inspiration upload.

02

Discover

“What should I wear? What’s the vibe?” — the stylist chat, look boards and occasion guides.

03

Decide

Narrow to two or three looks, match a designer, lock the palette. Video consults on demand.

04

Measure

Auto-measure, manual entry, tailor review, or a tape session on the next India trip.

05

Order

Landed, all-in cost. Delivery to the US or India. Pay in local currency.

06

Track

Cutting, stitching, embroidery, QC, dispatch — with real photos and estimated dates.

07

Approve & receive

Approve pre-dispatch photos, pick customs handling, unbox.

08

Remember

Wear, photograph, review, add to the wardrobe, restyle later.

The Aura loop. Stage 08 isn’t the end — every completed journey feeds the Fashion Graph and seeds Stage 01 of the next occasion.

The screens

Eight screens from the working prototype, each one answering a pillar from the specification.

01HomeOccasion-first

Moments, not product tiles

The front door asks what you’re dressing for, never what you want to buy. Editorial stories set the tone; your own calendar sits right under them.

  • Upcoming events — Navratri, a sister’s wedding in Chicago — carry their dates, so every look downstream is filtered by what can ship in time
  • Collections are grouped by occasion — Bridal, Festive, Cocktail — before silhouette
  • The “Ask me anything” bar is pinned to the bottom: text or voice, the stylist is always one tap away
02AI Personal DesignerSearch to conversation

From ten thousand products to three looks

“Tell me about the occasion — where it is, when it is, and how you want to feel in the room.” A few sharp exchanges later, the customer has a shortlist with a real path to purchase.

  • Suggested chips under every reply — “Review wardrobe”, “Suggest 9 looks” — so nobody is forced to type
  • Progressive disclosure: never more than two questions before a preview
  • Explainable curation: each look carries a “Why this?” line citing weather, saved palette and budget
  • Escalation to a human stylist when spend crosses $1,500 or the event is under three weeks away
03DiscoverAI inspiration

Taste before inventory

Curated collections, editorial stories and a designer spotlight — browse that feels like a magazine, one tap below the conversation.

  • Silhouette rail — Lehengas, Sarees, Anarkalis, Kurta sets — for customers who already know what they want
  • Editorial stories explain the craft (“The story behind a Banarasi”) rather than just showing it
  • Every tile routes back into the stylist, so inspiration becomes a priced, customizable order
04Discover designersVerified marketplace

The workshop behind the app

Aura warehouses nothing. It curates boutiques, artisans and weavers — and every one of them has a face, a city and a story.

  • Each card shows rating, NRI order count, specialty, city, lead time and whether it ships worldwide or is GI-certified
  • Ranked by fit-for-you and lead-time feasibility for your event — never by ad spend
  • Craft filters — Bridal, Handloom, Zardosi, Menswear — surface specialists first

Verification is an explainable checklist, not a badge: business & tax checks, a workshop visit, a sample audit, NRI shipping certification and a quarterly review.

05Curated collectionNRI-centric

Priced the way it lands

Collections are named for how people actually shop from abroad — “Under $400, fully landed”, “Packs flat, travels far”.

  • Filters by Occasion, Craft, Fit, Travel and Budget — the axes that decide an NRI order
  • Prices are landed and all-in: stitching, shipping, insurance and duties in one number, in the customer’s currency
  • The stylist bubble stays in reach to turn any piece into a conversation
06WardrobeDigital wardrobe & family

A closet that remembers — for the whole family

The wardrobe turns Aura from a shopping app into a relationship: every piece, every measurement, every person you shop for.

  • Sync calendar pulls occasion dates in and sets lead time on every commission
  • Measurement profiles — a primary set plus one per family member, each with its last update
  • Kids’ profiles flag “re-measure suggested — children grow”
  • An empty Favourites state speaks in Aura’s voice rather than a blank grid
07OrdersTrusted quality

Five glass rooms

Trust is Aura’s largest barrier to conversion, so the production tracker turns opacity into a feature.

  • In production — a stage tracker per piece (“Stage 4 of 8 · Handwork”) with the artisan, the city and a delivery date
  • Your approval — pre-dispatch photos wait for the customer before the box leaves
  • Group order — a wedding trousseau across family members, each with their own status
  • Delivery — “Duties prepaid — nothing to pay on arrival”
08Your profilePerfect fit guarantee

Fit as a promise, not a form

Fit is the number-one reason NRIs abandon Indian fashion sites. Measurements are saved once, reviewed by a real tailor, and backed by credits — not disclaimers.

  • Bust, waist, hip, shoulder, sleeve and length in inches or centimetres, with an AI scan as the low-effort path
  • Every AI estimate stays editable and shows its confidence
  • Phone is “used only for delivery and fitting calls” — privacy stated where it’s asked

The fit promise: measure your way · reviewed by a real tailor · photographed before dispatch · free first alteration · store credit if we fail.

Motion

  • 200 ms ease-out for surfaces, 280 ms for sheets, 400 ms for hero image swaps
  • Tracker milestones fill a gold hairline in 600 ms; the new photo cross-fades
  • Never animate prices, totals or countdowns — they update instantly to feel trustworthy
  • Reduced motion keeps only opacity fades

Accessibility

  • WCAG AA minimum — plum on ivory passes AAA; gold is decorative only
  • OS text scale respected up to 200%, tabular numerals keep prices aligned
  • Every status encoded in icon + colour + text, never colour alone
  • 44 × 44 pt touch targets; voice input on every text field

Designing for the hard days

Aura wins or loses on failure. Each scenario has a defined interaction pattern.

Scenario What Aura does
Event too close Offers rental partners, ready-made with express stitching, or blouse-only with an heirloom saree. Never shows a designer who can’t ship in time.
Auto-measure below 80% Routes to guided manual entry and offers a free 20-minute video with a tailor before confirming.
Family palette clash A “palette debate” card shows both preferences, three compromise palettes and a one-tap vote.
Boutique misses a milestone Proactive notice with options: accept the new date, switch designer, or cancel with a full refund plus $50 credit.
Damage on arrival One-tap “report a problem”, an instant alteration credit, and a prepaid return or local repair.
Language mix-up Every misread reply carries a “not what I meant” chip and an explicit language switch.
